# Sandwich instructions for my robot

## Assumptions
1. Robot starts with empty hands 
2. Robot has all ingredients unpackaged in front of him on a wooden table and is in front of the table
3. Ingredients include bread, turkey, cheese, mayo, and lettuce

## Instructions
1. Robot grabs 2 pieces of bread and lays them flat next to eachother
2. Robot then places 4 turkey pieces on the right piece of bread
3. Robot then places 2 pieces of cheese on the right piece bread
4. Robot then pours mayo on the right piece of bread for 4 seconds in a zig zag manner
5. Robot then places 4 pieces of lettuce on the right piece of bread 
6. Robot then places left piece of bread on top of the lettuce
## Done when
1. All ingredients are used 
2. Left piece of bread is on top of the lettuce
